Privacy Note

What we take.
What we don't.

Short version: we store your team sheet to run the bracket, never sell it, and purge it after the event.

What the form collects

  • Team name, team tag, in-game guild name
  • Captain IGN, BGMI Character ID (UID), email, phone, Discord handle (optional)
  • Each roster player's IGN and Character ID (4 starters, up to 2 substitutes)
  • Optional team logo image (PNG / JPG / WEBP, max 2 MB)

What happens automatically

  • Your IP address is held briefly for spam and rate-limit checks
  • A hidden honeypot field filters out form-filling bots
  • Requests carry a short-lived CSRF token — it dies after one use and is never stored with your squad

Why we store it

Only to seed the bracket, verify one-account-per-player, contact team captains about match times, and print the squad sheet on event day. That's it.

Who can see it

Tournament organizers only. The registration list sits behind an admin token — it is never exposed on any public page of this site.

What we never do

  • No selling, renting, or sharing your details with third parties
  • No marketing lists, no spam, no "partner offers"
  • No BGMI/Krafton affiliation — this data never leaves the tournament

Retention

Team sheets are purged automatically after the event's prize distribution closes. Logos are deleted with the squad they belong to. You can request early deletion any time before the event — just ask an organizer and we'll drop your row and cascade the roster.

Fair play & data

Character IDs are public-by-design identifiers inside BGMI — we use them to enforce one account per player. If a UID is already registered to another squad, both registrations are reviewed by organizers rather than silently overwritten.
