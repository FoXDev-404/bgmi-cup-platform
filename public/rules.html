HOUSE RULES

Tournament config

tournament = { mode: "SQUAD — TPP", maps: [ERANGEL, MIRAMAR, SANHOK], rounds: 6, entry: FREE, emulators: BANNED, emulation_tools: BANNED, accounts: ONE_PER_PLAYER, roster_changes: LOCKED_AFTER_CHECKIN, rage_quits: SKILL_ISSUE }
  • One account per player. Smurfs get the whole squad yeeted.
  • Emulator or emulation tool = instant disqualification. No appeals, no vibes-based defenses.
  • Check-in opens 60 minutes before lobby. Late squad = open slot.
  • Roster is locked at check-in. Your subs must be on the registered sheet. Surprise guests sit with us.
  • Toxicity, cheating, teaming with another squad: organizers' verdict is final. We have screenshots. We always have screenshots.

tl;dr — play fair, land smart, don't be weird.

THE FORMAT

Six matches. One chicken.

  • I — Qualifiers, 6 rounds: Erangel • Miramar • Sanhok rotation. TPP squad. Full points table.
  • II — Semifinals, top 16: Table toppers survive. The lobby gets hungry. The casting gets loud.
  • III — Grand Finals: Trophy, glory, stickers. In that order of importance. Allegedly.

Points table decides everything. Placement kills matter. So does not disconnecting mid-final.

FAIR PLAY

What you agree to when you register

  • My squad read the rules. No emulators, one account per player, roster locked at check-in.
  • We play fair. Organizers' verdict on cheating or toxicity is final.

Your team sheet (names, IGNs, UIDs, captain contact) is stored only to run the tournament — never sold, purged after the event. Privacy note
